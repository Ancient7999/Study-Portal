# Coronary Circulation

---

## Definition

**Coronary circulation** is blood flow between the heart and **its own muscular wall**. The right and left coronary arteries are the **first branches of the aorta**, so the myocardium is the first tissue to receive oxygenated blood from the left ventricle.

Named from **coron/o** (circle or crown): the arteries encircle the heart like a crown.

1. The **Left Ventricle** ejects oxygenated blood.
2. The **Aortic Semilunar Valve** opens into the aortic root.
3. At the root of the **Aorta**, the coronary arteries branch off immediately.
4. The **Coronary Arteries** spread over the heart surface.
5. They supply the **Myocardium**, which needs a constant oxygen supply to keep pumping.

### Main branches

| Artery | Typical branches / territory |
|--------|------------------------------|
| **Right coronary artery (RCA)** | Right marginal artery; in most people the **posterior descending artery (PDA)** |
| **Left coronary artery (LCA)** | Quickly divides into the **left anterior descending (LAD)** and the **circumflex** artery; diagonal branches leave the LAD |

A blockage in a coronary artery can cause a **myocardial infarction** (heart attack).
