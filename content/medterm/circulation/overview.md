# Circulation Overview

---

## Definition

The heart and blood vessels move blood in **three named circuits**:

| Circuit | Path | Blood |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Systemic circulation** | Heart ↔ body tissues | Left ventricle sends oxygenated blood out; venae cavae return deoxygenated blood to the right atrium |
| **Coronary circulation** | Heart ↔ its own wall | First branches of the aorta feed the myocardium |
| **Pulmonary circulation** | Heart ↔ lungs | Right ventricle sends deoxygenated blood to the lungs; pulmonary veins return oxygenated blood to the left atrium |

**Rule of sides:** the right heart handles **deoxygenated** blood; the left heart handles **oxygenated** blood. The pulmonary vessels are the exception to “arteries = oxygenated”: **pulmonary arteries** carry deoxygenated blood, **pulmonary veins** carry oxygenated blood.

## Whole heart at a glance

**Totals:** **4 chambers** · **4 valves** (2 atrioventricular + 2 semilunar).

### Chambers

| Chamber | Blood | Flows in through | Inlet valve | Opens into | Outlet valve | Opens into |
|---------|-------|------------------|-------------|------------|--------------|------------|
| **Right atrium (RA)** | Deoxygenated | Superior and inferior **vena cava** (no valve at the venous inlets) | — | — | **Tricuspid AV Valve** | Right ventricle |
| **Right ventricle (RV)** | Deoxygenated | Tricuspid AV Valve (from RA) | **Tricuspid AV Valve** | Right ventricle | **Pulmonary Semilunar Valve** | Pulmonary arteries → lungs |
| **Left atrium (LA)** | Oxygenated | **Pulmonary veins** (no valve at the venous inlets) | — | — | **Bicuspid AV Valve** (mitral) | Left ventricle |
| **Left ventricle (LV)** | Oxygenated | Bicuspid AV Valve (from LA) | **Bicuspid AV Valve** | Left ventricle | **Aortic Semilunar Valve** | Aorta → body (and coronary arteries) |

**Valve count by chamber:** each **ventricle has 2 valves** (one inlet AV valve and one outlet semilunar valve). Each **atrium has 1 valve** on its outlet into the ventricle below; the veins that empty into the atria have **no inlet valves** at the atrial wall.

### Valves

| Valve | Type | Cusps | Between | Opens toward | Closes to stop backflow into |
|-------|------|------:|---------|--------------|------------------------------|
| **Tricuspid AV Valve** | Atrioventricular (AV) | **3** | Right atrium → right ventricle | Right ventricle | Right atrium |
| **Pulmonary Semilunar Valve** | Semilunar | **3** | Right ventricle → pulmonary trunk | Pulmonary arteries | Right ventricle |
| **Bicuspid AV Valve** (mitral) | Atrioventricular (AV) | **2** | Left atrium → left ventricle | Left ventricle | Left atrium |
| **Aortic Semilunar Valve** | Semilunar | **3** | Left ventricle → aorta | Aorta | Left ventricle |

## Full loop (14 steps)

Blood leaves the body tissues and returns through the vena cava into the right atrium, crosses the tricuspid AV valve into the right ventricle, and is pumped through the pulmonary semilunar valve into the pulmonary arteries. After gas exchange in the lungs, pulmonary veins bring blood to the left atrium; it crosses the bicuspid AV valve into the left ventricle and leaves through the aortic semilunar valve into the aorta.

2. Open **Coronary Circulation** for the aortic branches that feed the myocardium.
3. Open **Pulmonary Circulation** for the right-ventricle → lungs → left-atrium path.
