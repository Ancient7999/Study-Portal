# Systemic Circulation

---

## Definition

**Systemic circulation** is the path between the heart and the **body tissues**. The left ventricle pumps oxygenated blood through the aortic semilunar valve into the aorta; after exchange in the tissues, deoxygenated blood returns through the **superior and inferior vena cava** into the right atrium.

1. **Left Ventricle** contracts and pushes oxygenated blood toward its outlet.
2. The **Aortic Semilunar Valve** opens into the aorta (3 cusps; closes to stop backflow into the LV).
3. The **Aorta** and its branches deliver blood to the body.
4. In the **Body Tissues**, oxygen is given up and carbon dioxide is picked up.
5. The **Vena Cava** (superior from the upper body, inferior from the lower body) returns deoxygenated blood.
6. Blood enters the **Right Atrium**, where systemic circulation ends and the pulmonary path begins next.

The left ventricle’s **inlet** is the **Bicuspid AV Valve** (2 cusps) from the left atrium; its **outlet** is the **Aortic Semilunar Valve** into the aorta — **2 valves** on this chamber.
