# Pulmonary Circulation

---

## Definition

**Pulmonary circulation** is the path between the heart and the **lungs**. The right ventricle pumps deoxygenated blood through the pulmonary semilunar valve into the pulmonary arteries; after gas exchange, the pulmonary veins return oxygenated blood to the left atrium.

1. The **Right Ventricle** contracts (inlet from the right atrium is the **Tricuspid AV Valve**, 3 cusps).
2. The **Pulmonary Semilunar Valve** (3 cusps) opens toward the pulmonary trunk.
3. The **Pulmonary Arteries** carry **deoxygenated** blood to the lungs — they come **before** the lungs.
4. In the **Lungs**, blood picks up oxygen and gives off carbon dioxide.
5. The **Pulmonary Veins** carry **oxygenated** blood back.
6. Blood enters the **Left Atrium**, where pulmonary circulation ends.

From the left atrium, blood next crosses the **Bicuspid AV Valve** into the left ventricle for systemic (and coronary) outflow.
