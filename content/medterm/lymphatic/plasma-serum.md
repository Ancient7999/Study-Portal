# Plasma and Serum

---

## Blood at a glance

Blood is liquid **plasma** plus **formed elements**: erythrocytes (red cells), leukocytes (white cells) and thrombocytes (platelets).

| Fraction | Share of blood volume | Contents |
|----------|-----------------------|----------|
| **Plasma** | About **55%** | Water, proteins, dissolved substances; carries the suspended cells |
| **Formed elements** | About **45%** | Red cells (most of it), white cells and platelets |

## Definitions

- **Plasma:** the yellowish **liquid part of blood**, still holding **fibrinogen** and the other **clotting factors**. Collected from blood mixed with an **anticoagulant**, so it never clots.
- **Serum:** the clear liquid **left after blood clots**: plasma **without** the clotting factors, which are used up in the clot.

**Key difference:** plasma keeps the clotting factors (fibrinogen); serum is what remains after clotting, so it has none.

## Properties

| Property | Plasma | Serum |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Definition** | Liquid part of blood | Liquid left after blood clots |
| **Clotting factors (fibrinogen)** | **Present** | **Absent** (used up in the clot) |
| **Collected from** | Anticoagulated blood (does not clot) | Clotted blood |
| **Share of blood** | About 55% of blood volume | Not measured as a share; separated from a clotted sample |
| **Water** | About 90–92% | About the same, with clotting proteins removed |
| **Proteins** | Albumin, globulins (antibodies) and fibrinogen | Albumin and globulins; no fibrinogen |
| **Other contents** | Electrolytes, nutrients (glucose), gases, hormones, wastes | The same dissolved substances |
| **Color** | Pale yellow (straw-colored) | Clear pale yellow |
| **Can it clot?** | Yes, if the anticoagulant is removed | No |
| **Typical use** | Transfusion of plasma, clotting tests | Antibody tests (serology), blood chemistry, antiserum for artificial passive immunity |

## Related body fluids

| Fluid | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Plasma** | Yellowish blood fraction with clotting factors |
| **Serum** | Liquid left after clotting |
| **Interstitial fluid** | Bathes cells outside the vessels |
| **Lymph** | Colorless liquid inside lymphatic vessels |

**Blood clotting = coagulation.** Thromb/o means clot, as in **thrombocyte** (platelet) and **thrombophlebitis** (inflammation of a vein caused by a clot).
