# Appendicular Skeleton

---

## Definition

The **appendicular skeleton** is the bones of the limbs and the girdles that attach them to the axial skeleton. The pectoral girdle links the upper limbs to the trunk; the pelvic girdle links the lower limbs and supports body weight.

**Textbook count: 126 bones.**

## Breakdown

| Region | Bones | Count |
|--------|-------|------:|
| Pectoral Girdle | Clavicle ×2, scapula ×2 | 4 |
| Upper limbs | Humerus ×2, ulna ×2, radius ×2, carpals 16, metacarpals 10, hand phalanges 28 | 60 |
| Pelvic Girdle | Hip bone (os coxae) ×2 | 2 |
| Lower limbs | Femur ×2, patella ×2, tibia ×2, fibula ×2, tarsals 14, metatarsals 10, foot phalanges 28 | 60 |
| **Total** | | **126** |

## Key points

- Each upper limb has **30** bones (1+1+1+8+5+14); both sides = **60**.
- Each lower limb has **30** bones (1+1+1+1+7+5+14); both sides = **60**.
- The adult **hip bone** is one fused bone per side (ilium + ischium + pubis), so the pelvic girdle counts as **2**, not 6.
- Shape filter tip: limb shafts and digit bones are **long**; carpals and tarsals are **short**; scapula is **flat**; hip bone is **irregular**; patella is **sesamoid**.

The **pectoral girdle** (shoulder girdle) is the pair of bones on each side that attaches the upper limb to the axial skeleton. It has **4 bones**: the **clavicle** (long bone) and **scapula** (flat bone) on each side.

- **Connects:** the upper limb to the axial skeleton at the **sternum**, through the **sternoclavicular joint** (clavicle to manubrium). This is the girdle's only joint with the axial skeleton.
- Within the girdle, the clavicle meets the scapula at the **acromioclavicular joint**; the humerus sits in the scapula's shallow **glenoid cavity** (shoulder joint).
- The scapula is held mainly by muscles, not by a joint with the ribs or spine.

The **pelvic girdle** (hip girdle) is the pair of hip bones that attaches the lower limbs to the axial skeleton. It has **2 bones**: one **hip bone** (os coxae, irregular bone) on each side.

- Each hip bone is **one fused bone** formed from the **ilium**, **ischium** and **pubis**, which meet at the acetabulum (hip socket).
- **Connects:** the lower limb to the axial skeleton at the **sacrum**, through the two **sacroiliac joints**. At the front, the two hip bones meet each other at the **pubic symphysis**.
- The two hip bones with the sacrum and coccyx form the **bony pelvis**; the sacrum and coccyx themselves are axial bones.

## Pectoral vs pelvic girdle

| | Pectoral Girdle | Pelvic Girdle |
|--|--|--|
| Bones | Clavicle + scapula per side (4) | Hip bone per side (2) |
| Joint with axial skeleton | Sternoclavicular (sternum) | Sacroiliac (sacrum) |
| Front connection | None between the two sides | Pubic symphysis |
| Built for | **Mobility**: light, loosely attached, shallow socket | **Stability** and **weight-bearing**: heavy, firmly joined, deep socket |
