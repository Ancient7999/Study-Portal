## Reused assets (referenced, not copied)

- **MT_lec12-13 blood flow (heart-flow):** our own SVG drawing (`heart-flow-labeled.svg` / `heart-flow-blank.svg`), adapted from the quiz's blood-flow ordering animation. No outside image; it replaces the old q1 course-image picture. Dashed blue (deoxygenated) and red (oxygenated) lines move along the vessels in the direction of flow; the `-slow.svg` twins run at one-third speed for Basic Quality, and reduced motion stops the animation.
- **MT_lec12-13 heart q2 to q5:** the blank images are `../heart-label-assets/qN.png`, and their credits are in `/workspace/heart-label-assets/CREDITS.md`. q2 is your own course material and is **not openly licensed**, so use it only inside the course. q3 to q5 are CC BY-SA 3.0. The labeled companions in `MT_lec12-13/qN-labeled.png` are new. For q2, the labeled version is the original course image with the same crop. For q3 to q5, the answers are drawn into the blank label areas.
- **MT_lec11 single-bone images (21):** these are `../bone-assets/img/*.png`, with credits in `/workspace/bone-assets/CREDITS.md`. They are BodyParts3D/Anatomography (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP), except the patella (Patrick J. Lynch, CC BY 2.5). They have no labeled version and no hotspots; each has one answer.
